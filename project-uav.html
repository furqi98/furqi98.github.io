UAV Research Platform: A Physical Platform for Onboard AI

I built and integrated a 550 mm quadcopter as a platform for testing onboard AI and autonomous flight.

The system uses a CUAV X7 Pro running ArduPilot, a Jetson Orin NX companion computer, an Intel RealSense RGB-D camera and a SIYI video and telemetry link. The Jetson communicates with the flight controller through MAVSDK, records RGB-D data and runs vision models onboard. I tested the platform from indoor hover through autonomous survey missions.

System architecture

The flight controller handles stabilization and mission execution, while the Jetson handles perception and higher-level decisions. The two communicate over MAVLink.

Onboard software

What Runs on the Vehicle

01Flight control

ArduPilot on the CUAV X7 Pro, with missions planned and monitored in QGroundControl.

02Offboard control

MAVSDK on the Jetson sends commands to the flight controller over MAVLink.

03RGB-D capture

A RealSense pipeline on the Jetson records synchronised colour and depth frames.

04Onboard inference

Vision and vision-language models run on the Jetson, with video and telemetry sent to the ground over the SIYI link.

Running the full Survey-RAG and Hexagrid loops on this vehicle is future work.

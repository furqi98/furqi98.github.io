Survey-RAG: Language-Queryable Aerial Memory
Aerial autonomy · open-vocabulary retrieval · AirSim + PX4 · CPU-only
A drone surveys an area once, turns everything it sees into a searchable spatial memory, and afterwards answers plain-language requests by retrieving the matching view and flying to its recorded coordinates. 0.93 P@1 on a hand-labelled benchmark; 7 of 8 typed requests flown correctly in a single AirSim + PX4 SITL closed-loop flight.

Teaching a Robot to Pick and Place Through Demonstration
Imitation learning · ACT · SmolVLA · Hugging Face LeRobot
A physical study of how demonstration structure affects imitation learning. Using LeRobot and a SO-101 leader–follower arm, I ran three progressively structured data-collection experiments. The final dataset covered 19 distinct locations, with four orientations demonstrated at every location (76 configurations). The ACT policy was reliable within that demonstrated region but did not reliably generalize to unseen regions.

Hexagrid VLM: Autonomous Landing-Site Selection
VLM-in-the-loop control · AirSim + PX4 SITL · Qwen2.5-VL, local
Turning a VLM’s visual judgment into an executable landing decision. Six views are compared in a single inference, repeated grid votes lock onto one cell rather than trusting one answer, and camera geometry turns that cell into a GPS target for an automatic descent. Demonstrated in simulation with PX4 SITL and AirSim.

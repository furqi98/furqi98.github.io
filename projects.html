Survey-RAG: Language-Queryable Aerial Memory
Aerial autonomy · open-vocabulary retrieval · AirSim + PX4 · CPU-only
A drone surveys an area once, turns everything it sees into a searchable spatial memory, and afterwards answers plain-language requests by retrieving the matching view and flying to its recorded coordinates. 0.93 P@1 on a hand-labelled benchmark; 7 of 8 typed requests flown correctly in a single closed-loop flight.

SO-101: Learning Manipulation from Demonstration
Imitation learning · vision-language-action · LeRobot + Isaac Sim
A full learning-from-demonstration stack on a physical SO-101 arm: teleoperated data collection, seven policies across three architectures, and three rounds of data collection that each changed where demonstrations were sampled from. Concentrating demonstrations in a single region beat spreading nearly twice as many frames across the whole workspace — demonstration density, not episode count, was the binding constraint.

Hexagrid VLM: Autonomous Landing-Site Selection
VLM-in-the-loop control · AirSim + PX4 SITL · Qwen2.5-VL, local
A drone flies a hexagon over unknown terrain and asks a vision-language model which way is safest to land. Six views are compared in a single inference, a confidence loop locks onto one grid cell rather than trusting one answer, and the cell becomes a GPS target for a fully automatic descent — no operator step anywhere in the sequence.

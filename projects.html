Survey-RAG: Language-Queryable Aerial Memory

Aerial autonomy · open-vocabulary retrieval · AirSim + PX4 · CPU-only

A UAV surveys an area once and stores the captured images with their locations. Later, an operator can use a natural-language query to find a relevant image and send the UAV back to that location. The system achieved 0.93 P@1 on a human-labelled benchmark and 7/8 successful retrieval-to-flight requests in AirSim + PX4 SITL.

Teaching a Robot to Pick and Place Through Demonstration

Imitation learning · ACT · SmolVLA · Hugging Face LeRobot

I studied how the way demonstrations are collected affects imitation learning on a SO-101 robot arm. I ran three data-collection experiments with different levels of workspace coverage and trained ACT policies on the resulting datasets. The final dataset covered 19 locations with four orientations at each location, but the policy still struggled to generalize beyond the demonstrated region.

Hexagrid VLM: Autonomous Landing-Site Selection

VLM-in-the-loop control · AirSim + PX4 SITL · Qwen2.5-VL, local

I built a VLM-based landing-site selection system for a simulated UAV. The system compares six camera views, uses repeated 4×4 grid predictions to select a suitable landing area, and uses camera geometry to convert the selected cell into a GPS target for descent.

UAV Research Platform: A Physical Platform for Onboard AI

Hardware integration · ArduPilot · Jetson Orin NX · RealSense

I built a 550 mm quadcopter as a platform for testing onboard AI and autonomous flight. It uses a CUAV X7 Pro, Jetson Orin NX, RealSense RGB-D camera and MAVSDK, with ArduPilot handling the flight controller. I tested the platform from manual flight through autonomous survey missions.

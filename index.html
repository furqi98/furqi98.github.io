Syed Furqan Ali

Hi, I’m Syed Furqan Ali. I work on Embodied AI, building systems that connect multimodal perception and language-based reasoning to action in the physical world. My recent work spans aerial robotics and physical manipulation: language-queryable drone navigation in Survey-RAG, VLM-guided landing selection in Hexagrid, and learning manipulation policies on a physical SO-101 arm.

SO-101 Robot Learning

Learning pick-and-place manipulation from teleoperated demonstrations on a physical SO-101 arm, from data collection through policy training to deployment.

Survey-RAG

A drone surveys an environment once, turning what it sees into a searchable spatial memory it can later search with natural-language requests.

0.93 P@1 on a 44-query benchmark · 7/8 closed-loop requests in AirSim + PX4 SITL

Details Video

Hexagrid

A vision-language model evaluates candidate landing areas from six views in a single inference and grounds its choice into a metric flight target for a fully autonomous descent.

Six views compared per decision · no operator step anywhere in the loop

Details Video

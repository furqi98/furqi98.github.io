Syed Furqan Ali

I work on robotics and embodied AI — building systems that take instructions in plain language and act on them under real constraints: limited onboard compute, a finite battery, and no assumption of connectivity.

My recent work includes Survey-RAG, a drone that surveys an area once and afterwards answers requests like "go to the park with the fountain" by retrieving the matching aerial view and flying to it; and an imitation-learning stack on the SO-101 manipulator that learns pick-and-place from teleoperated demonstration. I care as much about measuring these systems honestly as about building them.

I am currently applying for PhD positions in embodied AI and robot learning.

Embodied AI projects

Research interests

  • Vision-language-action policies — what actually transfers between architectures, pretraining and data
  • Open-vocabulary spatial memory — letting a robot answer questions about a place when the question was unknown at capture time
  • Foundation models under embodied constraints — which competence belongs at which latency and energy tier
  • Learning from demonstration — what makes a demonstration dataset good rather than merely large
  • Evaluation methodology — benchmarks that can actually be wrong, and ground-truth hygiene
  • Aerial autonomy for search and rescue — one flight, no connectivity, unknown queries

Syed Furqan Ali

Hi, I’m Syed Furqan Ali. I work on Embodied AI, building systems that connect perception and language-based reasoning to action in the physical world. I have been exploring this through several research projects, including language-queryable drone navigation in Survey-RAG and VLM-guided safe landing area selection in an unknown environment in Hexagrid. I have also been collecting teleoperated demonstration datasets for training an SO-101 follower arm, where I trained an ACT policy that successfully performs pick-and-place tasks.

Research direction

Building multimodal systems that perceive, reason, and act across aerial and manipulation robotics.

Embodied AI projects

Survey-RAG

A drone surveys an environment once, turning what it sees into a searchable spatial memory it can later search with natural-language requests.

0.93 P@1 on a 44-query benchmark · 7/8 retrieval-to-flight requests flown correctly

Details Video

Hexagrid

A vision-language model evaluates candidate landing areas from six views in a single inference and grounds its choice into a GPS flight target for a fully autonomous descent.

Six views compared per decision · no operator step anywhere in the loop

Details Video

SO-101 Robot Learning

Learning pick-and-place manipulation from teleoperated demonstrations on a physical SO-101 arm, from data collection through policy training to deployment.

7/8 successful pick-and-place trials · 76 demonstrations, 30,110 frames released publicly

Recent research

Aug 2026 — Survey-RAG

Completed evaluation of an open-vocabulary UAV spatial-memory system, achieving 0.93 P@1 on a 44-query benchmark and 7/8 successful natural-language retrieval-to-flight requests.

Jun 2026 — Hexagrid

Developed an end-to-end VLM-guided UAV landing pipeline that compares six candidate views in a single inference and converts the selected landing region into a metric flight target.

May 2026 — Physical Robot Learning

Trained and deployed an ACT policy on a physical SO-101 arm, achieving 7/8 successful pick-and-place trials, and released the associated teleoperation dataset on Hugging Face.

Workshop and field

I build, assemble, configure, and test the systems I work with, from 3D-printing and assembling the SO-101 robotic arm to building a 550mm carbon-fiber UAV. This includes integrating onboard computing, power systems, communication hardware, and sensors such as NVIDIA Jetson platforms, buck-boost converters, SIYI air units, and Intel RealSense cameras before taking the systems into the field.
